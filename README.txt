ATTENDANCE BOOK - how to share it with colleagues
==================================================

What this is: a small app (a web app that installs like a normal app).
It works offline. Each person's entries are saved on their own phone only.

STEP 1: Put the files online (once, on a computer)
  Easiest: Netlify Drop
    1. Open app.netlify.com/drop and sign in (a free account is enough).
    2. Unzip this file, then drag the whole folder onto the page.
    3. Netlify gives you a link (https://...). Copy it.
  Other free options: GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages (upload the same files).

STEP 2: Install on each phone (you and your colleagues)
    1. Open the link in Chrome on Android.
    2. Tap the three-dot menu, then "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen").
    3. Open "Attendance" from the home screen. It now works like an app, even offline.

STEP 3: First use
    Enter your per-day salary and OT rate once under Salary. Tap a date to enter OT or absent.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - Entries live on that phone. Clearing Chrome's site data or uninstalling the app erases them.
  - To update the app later, upload the new files to the same site and reopen the app.
  - Optional real APK file: go to pwabuilder.com, enter your link, choose Android, and download the package.
    An APK can be installed from a file without the Play Store.
